Account & Data Deletion Request

At THE LOCALS (available on Android, iOS, and Web), we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by emailing our support team with your registered phone number or email address. Our team will verify and complete your deletion within 7 business days.

What Data Will Be Deleted:

  • User Profile: Name, email address, phone number, bio, state, city, and profile picture.
  • Authentication Credentials: Firebase login credentials, session tokens, and security records.
  • Host Data (for Guides): Created itineraries, PDF brochures, uploaded gallery photos, and guide verification records.
  • Traveler Data: Wishlists, reviews, and travel preferences.

Data Retention Policy:

Financial transaction and invoice records related to completed Razorpay bookings will be retained for up to 180 days solely for regulatory compliance, anti-fraud verification, and legal accounting purposes as required by law, after which they are permanently purged.
